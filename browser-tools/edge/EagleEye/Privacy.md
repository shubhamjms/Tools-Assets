# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to this browser extension.

## No Personal Data Collection

We respect your privacy. We do not collect personal or sensitive user data through this extension.

We do not receive or retain your name, email address, browsing history, website content, passwords, payment information, or other personal information from your use of the extension.

## No Tracking or Data Sharing

We do not use the extension to track your browsing activity, build user profiles, or collect personal information through analytics or advertising tools.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data obtained through the extension, because we do not collect it.

## Browser Permissions

If the extension requests browser permissions, those permissions are used only to provide its functionality. Access required for a feature does not mean that personal data is collected by or transmitted to us.

## Third-Party Services

This policy covers the extension only. Your browser, the extension store, and websites you visit have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

## Changes to This Policy

If our data-handling practices change, we will update this policy and its "Last updated" date before those changes take effect.
